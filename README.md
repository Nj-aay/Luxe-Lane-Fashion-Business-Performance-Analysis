LuxeLane Fashion 
Business Performance Analysis

Overview
A business performance analysis conducted to understand LuxeLane Fashion's decline in revenue and profitability during H2 2025.

Business Problem
Revenue declined by 23.4% while profit declined by 50.8% in H2. The analysis investigates the factors behind this decline.

Objectives
Analyse revenue and profit trends
Compare H1 vs H2 performance
Evaluate product profitability
Analyse customer acquisition channels
Examine complaints, returns and delivery performance
Provide actionable recommendations

Tools
Microsoft Excel
PivotTables
Excel formulas
Data visualisation
Business analysis

Key Findings
Revenue: ₦41.87M
Profit: ₦11.17M
H2 revenue decline: 23.4%
H2 profit decline: 50.8%
H2 discounts increased significantly
Average delivery time increased from 2.8 to 4.6 days
Instagram generated the highest channel revenue
Product profitability varied significantly

ROOT CAUSE

Based on the analysis, LUXELANE'S decline appears to be the result of several issues occurring simultaneously, rather than one isolated problem.
The major issues identified were:
1. Declining revenue
Sales weakened considerably during H2.
2. Increasing discounts
Average discount increased substantially, putting pressure on margins. 
3. Uneven product profitability
Some products generated strong revenue but were not necessarily the strongest profit contributors.
4. Customer experience issues
Complaints and returns indicate that some customers experienced problems with their orders and that’s the reason for decline in sales in H2
5. Delivery deterioration
Average delivery time increased significantly during H2.

Recommendations
1. Review the discount strategy
Instead of applying broad discounts across products, LUXELANE should identify the products and customer segments where discounts actually generate enough additional sales to justify the reduction in margin. Reduce discounts or remove entirely because its not making customers buy more
Recommendation: Track profit after discount, not revenue alone.
2. Focus marketing on profitable products
Products with strong profit contribution should receive greater attention in marketing campaigns. LUXELANE should avoid assuming that its best-selling product is automatically its most valuable product.

3. Investigate delivery problems
Management should investigate why delivery times increased during H2.
Areas to review include:
•	Delivery partners 
•	Order processing time 
•	Inventory availability 
•	Geographic coverage 
•	Peak-period capacity 
4. Reduce avoidable returns and complaints
Products with unusually high return or complaint rates should be investigated.
LUXELANE could review:
•	Product descriptions 
•	Size information 
•	Product quality 
•	Product photos 
•	Customer expectations 
5. Measure customer retention
The business should track customers beyond their first purchase.
Important KPIs include:
•	Repeat purchase rate 
•	Customer lifetime value 
•	Average order value 
•	Revenue per customer 
•	Repeat purchases by acquisition channel 
6. Temporarily shutdown walk-in store and operate from factory or home so the business is strictly online to reduce cost of rent and increase net profit.

CONCLUSION

LUXELANE'S 2025 performance shows that the business doesn't have a simple sales problem. It has a broader profitability and operational problem.
Revenue declined by approximately 23% in H2, while profit declined by approximately 51%. During the same period, discounts increased considerably and delivery performance weakened.
The biggest opportunity is therefore not simply to "get more customers."
